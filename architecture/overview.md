# NEXUS – Architecture Overview

## 1. Purpose

NEXUS is a fictional digital banking and payments platform designed as an independent educational and architecture portfolio project.

The purpose of this architecture is to demonstrate how modern banking and payment systems can be designed, documented, integrated, secured, and deployed using established software architecture practices.

## 2. System of Interest

The system of interest is the NEXUS Digital Banking & Payments Platform.

NEXUS will simulate selected capabilities commonly found in digital banking and payment ecosystems, including:

- Customer and account management
- Digital wallets and balances
- Fund transfers
- Merchant payments
- Transaction processing and status tracking
- Notifications
- Audit logging and reporting

All transactions will use synthetic data and simulated integrations.

## 3. Architecture Objectives

The architecture aims to:

1. Demonstrate clear separation of business, application, data, integration, security, and deployment concerns.
2. Define system boundaries and interactions with external systems.
3. Document important architecture decisions and their rationale.
4. Demonstrate API-first design using OpenAPI.
5. Support a working prototype that illustrates selected banking and payment workflows.
6. Publish architecture documentation and demonstrations online.

## 4. Architecture Approach

The NEXUS architecture documentation will be guided by ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 concepts, including:

- Stakeholders
- Architecture concerns
- Architecture viewpoints and views
- Architecture models
- Architecture decisions and rationale

The C4 model will be used to communicate software architecture at different levels of detail.

## 5. Planned Architecture Views

The project will document:

- **System Context:** NEXUS and its relationships with users and external systems.
- **Business Architecture:** Business capabilities, processes, and actors.
- **Application Architecture:** Major software components and responsibilities.
- **Data Architecture:** Data entities, ownership, and movement.
- **Integration Architecture:** APIs, events, and system interactions.
- **Security Architecture:** Authentication, authorization, and security controls.
- **Deployment Architecture:** Runtime environments and infrastructure.

These are project-specific documentation views, not a mandatory set prescribed by ISO 42010.

## 6. Architecture Principles

The initial principles are:

- API-first design
- Security by design
- Clear separation of responsibilities
- Traceable architecture decisions
- Observability and auditability
- Simplicity before unnecessary complexity
- Technology choices justified by requirements

## 7. Assumptions and Constraints

- NEXUS is not a production banking system.
- All customers, accounts, balances, and transactions are fictional.
- External banking and payment networks will be simulated.
- No proprietary employer documentation or confidential information will be used.
- The initial implementation will prioritize learning, clarity, and manageable scope.

## 8. Current Status

The project is in its initial architecture definition phase.

Stakeholders, concerns, requirements, architecture decisions, diagrams, and prototype functionality will be developed incrementally.
